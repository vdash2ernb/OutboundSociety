## About

### About Outbound Society
**Running for a Better Tomorrow.**

#### Who We Are

Outbound Society is a revolutionary non-profit event organizer creating intentional, inclusive, and meaningful running experiences while breaking the norms of traditional running events. Founded by Roefan Jugalbot and based in Purok Ube, Catarman, Liloan, Cebu, we bring communities together through the power of movement, wellness, and genuine human connection.

Whether you are stepping into your very first community run, enjoying a weekend walk, or training for a personal best, Outbound Society provides a welcoming space for runners and fitness enthusiasts of all levels across Liloan, Consolacion, Compostela, Mandaue, Danao, and the greater Cebu area.

#### Our Mission & Goal

Our goal is simple: create more opportunities for people to move, meet new friends, and enjoy running together.

We believe running should be accessible, exciting, and rooted in community spirit. Instead of traditional, high-pressure races, we design memorable events focused on community impact, intentional routes, and shared milestones.

#### Our Non-Profit Promise

We are driven by community growth, not profits.

Every peso we earn from our events goes right back into organizing future runs and growing our community.

By participating in an Outbound Society event, you are directly investing in the growth of local sports initiatives, community wellness programs, and safer, better-organized runs across Cebu North.

#### What We Do

Beyond organizing our own signature community runs and sports gatherings, Outbound Society offers end-to-end event planning and digital marketing services:

- **Community Runs & Fitness Activations:** Creative, family-friendly, and inclusive running events designed for all fitness levels.
- **Event Management & Strategy:** Complete planning, logistics, and execution for local brand activations, sports gatherings, and non-profit initiatives across Cebu.
- **Digital Marketing & Growth:** Amplifying local wellness movements and grassroots events through targeted digital campaigns.
